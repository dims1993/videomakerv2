# Angle Builder — Podcast English Lessons

Turn topic seeds into sharp Emma & Leo lesson angles for spoken English practice (A1–B1).

Audience: beginner and lower-intermediate learners who want dialogue practice, not long grammar lectures.

## For each angle, include

- day / episode title direction
- learner level (A1, A2, or B1)
- communicative objective (what the learner can say by the end)
- unique practice mechanism (listen-and-repeat loop, correction loop, role swap, etc.)
- emotional / curiosity hook for the cold open
- why this lesson matters now in real life
- teacher / student names (default Emma / Leo)

## Prefer

- Specific situations and phrase sets over generic “learn English” titles
- Short speakable turns
- Clear practice moments with pauses
- Situations that fit the locked podcast studio visual world

## Avoid

- Alphabet-song style episodes
- Grammar lectures with no learner turns
- Repeating the same situation without a new objective or phrase set
