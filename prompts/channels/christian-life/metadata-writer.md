# Christian Life — Metadata Writer

## Purpose

This file defines how to create YouTube metadata for the Christian Life channel.

The Metadata Writer transforms a video angle or script into:

- strong titles
- descriptions
- thumbnail text
- tags
- pinned comment
- hashtags
- chapter ideas
- SEO keywords
- emotional positioning
- viewer promise

The goal is to package biblical life advice videos in a way that is:

- emotionally clickable
- spiritually respectful
- simple to understand
- clear for the viewer
- searchable on YouTube
- aligned with the channel mission
- not manipulative
- not fear-based
- not prosperity-focused

The metadata should help the right viewer understand:

**“This video speaks to something I am living right now.”**

---

## Core Metadata Formula

Every metadata package should be built around:

```text
HUMAN PAIN + BIBLICAL PRINCIPLE + EMOTIONAL PROMISE + PRACTICAL HOPE
```

The metadata should not sell abstract theology.

It should package a human struggle through biblical clarity.

# Title Strategy

The title should be:

- emotionally specific
- simple
- curiosity-driven
- connected to a real life problem
- connected to biblical wisdom
- understandable to believers and spiritually curious viewers
- respectful
- not manipulative

A good title should make the viewer feel:

```text
This might help me understand something I am carrying.
```

---

# Title Quality Rules

## Good Titles

Good titles usually have:

- emotional tension
- clear life problem
- biblical relevance
- curiosity
- simplicity
- no overpromising

Examples:

```text
The Hidden Cost Of Refusing To Forgive
```

```text
You Can Forgive Without Pretending It Did Not Hurt
```

```text
When Waiting Feels Like God Is Silent
```

```text
Why Pride Builds Walls
```

```text
The Bible’s Answer To Anxiety
```

```text
Why Comparison Steals Your Peace
```

```text
When Guilt Makes You Hide
```

```text
Faith Does Not Always Show The Whole Road
```

---

## Weak Titles

Avoid generic titles like:

```text
Forgiveness Is Important
```

```text
Bible Study About Patience
```

```text
Christian Advice For Life
```

```text
How To Be Better
```

```text
God Loves You
```

These may be true, but they are too broad and emotionally weak.

---

## Clickbait To Avoid

Avoid titles that promise too much:

```text
Watch This And God Will Fix Everything
```

```text
This One Verse Will Change Your Life Overnight
```

```text
If You See This, God Is Sending You A Sign
```

```text
God Will Punish Them For Hurting You
```

```text
Do This And Your Breakthrough Is Guaranteed
```

```text
Real Christians Never Feel Anxiety
```

The channel should never use fear, manipulation, or false promises to get clicks.

# Thumbnail Strategy

The thumbnail should show:

```text
MAIN STICKMAN + ONE BIG SYMBOLIC ELEMENT + ONE CLEAR EMOTION
```

The thumbnail should be understandable in less than two seconds.

Use the stickman as the emotional anchor.

Use one symbolic object to represent the principle.

---

## Thumbnail Text Rules

Use very little text.

Best options:

- 0–2 words
- 3 words maximum
- large and readable
- emotionally strong
- no tiny Bible references
- no long phrases

# Description Strategy

The description should do four things:

1. identify the human struggle
2. introduce the biblical principle
3. explain what the viewer will learn
4. invite reflection without pressure

The first two lines matter most.

They should be emotionally clear and searchable.

---

## Description Template

```text
{opening_emotional_line}

In this illustrated Christian Life video, we explore {topic} through a simple visual story about {human_pain}. Using biblical wisdom, this video shows how {biblical_principle} and why {wrong_response} can keep the heart trapped.

You will see how {visual_metaphor} represents {life_problem}, and how one small choice can begin the movement from {starting_state} to {transformed_state}.

This video is for anyone who feels {viewer_state} and needs a simple reminder that there is still light for this part of life.

Topics covered:
- {topic_1}
- {topic_2}
- {topic_3}
- {topic_4}
- {topic_5}

If this video helped you, consider subscribing for more illustrated biblical life lessons, Christian wisdom, and simple visual stories about faith, character, hope, and daily life.
```

---

```text
Sometimes the heaviest thing you carry is not what happened to you. It is what you refuse to release.

In this illustrated Christian Life video, we explore forgiveness through a simple visual story about carrying bitterness, replaying old wounds, and learning what biblical forgiveness really means. Using biblical wisdom, this video shows why forgiveness is not pretending the wound did not matter, but refusing to let bitterness keep controlling your heart.

You will see how a heavy chain represents unforgiveness, and how one small choice can begin the movement from bitterness to release.

This video is for anyone who has been hurt, betrayed, disappointed, or stuck replaying something they wish they could finally let go of.

Topics covered:

- letting go of bitterness

- emotional healing
- forgiveness and peace

If this video helped you, consider subscribing for more illustrated biblical life lessons, Christian wisdom, and simple visual stories about faith, character, hope, and daily life.
```

# Metadata Tone Rules

The metadata should sound:

- warm
- clear
- emotional
- helpful
- biblical
- hopeful
- accessible

It should not sound:

- preachy
- aggressive
- manipulative
- dramatic in a fake way
- prosperity-focused
- shame-based
- politically charged
- like religious clickbait

---

# Metadata Avoid List

Avoid:

- fear-based clickbait
- prosperity gospel framing
- fake prophetic urgency
- “God sent you this video”
- “You must watch before it is too late”
- “This is your sign”
- “God will punish your enemies”
- “Do this and receive your breakthrough”
- “Real Christians never struggle”
- “One verse will fix your life”
- political framing
- denominational arguments
- attacking other groups
- guilt manipulation
- excessive capitalization
- excessive emojis
- excessive hashtags
- misleading titles
- titles unrelated to the video
- titles that overpromise healing, money, success, or instant transformation

# Metadata Quality Checklist

Before approving metadata, check:

- Is the title emotionally specific?
- Is the title searchable?
- Is the title respectful?
- Does the title avoid false promises?
- Does the title avoid fear-based manipulation?
- Does the thumbnail concept show the main stickman?
- Does the thumbnail have one big symbolic element?
- Is the thumbnail text short?
- Does the description explain the human pain?
- Does the description explain the biblical principle?
- Does the description avoid overpromising?
- Are tags relevant?
- Are hashtags limited and useful?
- Does the pinned comment invite meaningful reflection?
- Does the package feel hopeful?
- Does the package align with the Project Bible?

If several answers are no, rewrite the metadata.

---

# Final Rule

Christian Life metadata should never feel like:

```text
Click this because you are afraid.
```

It should feel like:

```text
This may bring light to something you are carrying.
```
