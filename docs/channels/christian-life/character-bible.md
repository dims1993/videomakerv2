# Stickman Channel - Character Bible

## Purpose

This file defines the character universe for the Stickman Channel.

The visual system is simple:

**MAIN STICKMAN + BIG EXPLANATORY ELEMENTS**

The main stickman is the constant visual anchor of the channel.

Every image should include the main stickman by default unless there is a rare, explicitly justified exception.

Supporting stickman characters may appear, but they expand the scene instead of replacing the main stickman.

Character design must be described through concrete visual traits, not through named shows, named characters, direct recognizable character references, or IP-adjacent wording.

---

## Character Universe

All stickman characters should belong to this shared visual universe:

"minimal emotional stickman storytelling style, simple black stick figures with round white heads, two small black dot eyes, expressive black eyebrows, tiny simple mouths, no noses, no hair, no ears, thin black stick bodies, clean 2D hand-drawn linework, slightly organic lines, minimal props, soft white or light neutral backgrounds, strong emotional readability, simple symbolic storytelling compositions"

Shared universe traits:

- minimal emotional stickman storytelling style
- simple black stick figure bodies
- round white heads
- two small black dot eyes
- expressive black eyebrows
- tiny simple mouths
- no noses
- no hair
- no ears
- thin black stick arms and legs
- clean 2D hand-drawn linework
- slightly organic black lines
- minimal props
- strong emotional readability
- simple readable silhouettes
- soft white or light neutral backgrounds
- simple symbolic storytelling compositions

Stickman characters may vary, but they should still feel like they belong to the same channel world.

---

## Main Stickman

The main stickman is the recognizable brand/channel character.

He should appear in every scene by default.

The main stickman may be:

- large in the foreground
- medium-sized beside the visual idea
- smaller when a large symbol or object needs to dominate
- facing the viewer
- pointing at an element
- reacting to an element
- physically participating in the metaphor
- standing beside another stickman
- guiding the viewer through a simple visual explanation
- emotionally reacting to the situation
- trapped by, pushed by, holding, resisting, or observing a symbolic element

Main stickman descriptor:

"main recurring emotional stickman from the shared Stickman Channel universe, simple black stick figure body, perfectly round white head, two small black dot eyes, expressive black eyebrow strokes, tiny simple mouth capable of showing emotion, no nose, no hair, no ears, thin black stick arms and legs, simple rounded head larger than the body, clean 2D hand-drawn linework, slightly organic black lines, minimal readable silhouette, emotionally vulnerable but relatable personality, strong body language, soft white or light neutral background"

Use this descriptor in main stickman image prompts.

Keep the main stickman visually consistent.

The main stickman face silhouette should be specific:

- perfectly round white head
- head slightly larger than the body
- two small black dot eyes
- simple expressive black eyebrows
- tiny simple mouth
- no nose
- no hair
- no ears
- no neck
- head visually connected to the stick body
- thin black stick body
- thin black stick arms and legs

Do not generate realistic eyes, white cartoon eyes, pupils, eyelashes, noses, ears, hair, clothing, facial hair, visible necks, realistic hands, or detailed feet.

The main stickman should remain extremely simple and recognizable.

---

## Main Stickman Personality

The main stickman should feel like:

- an everyday person
- emotionally honest
- slightly vulnerable
- thoughtful
- relatable
- observant
- quietly expressive
- intelligent but not arrogant
- stressed when the story requires it
- hopeful when the story turns toward clarity
- human despite being visually minimal

The main stickman should not feel like:

- a generic app mascot
- a hyperactive cartoon mascot
- a superhero
- a meme character
- a luxury finance influencer
- a corporate stock character
- a realistic human
- an anime character
- a childish doodle without emotional intention

---

## Supporting Stickman Characters

Supporting stickman characters are secondary people who may appear beside the main stickman when the narration benefits from another human example.

Supporting stickman characters should not replace the main stickman as the visual anchor.

Supporting characters expand the scene, but the main stickman anchors the channel identity.

Supporting stickman descriptor:

"supporting stickman character from the same Stickman Channel universe, simple black stick figure body, round white head, two small black dot eyes, simple expressive black eyebrows, tiny simple mouth, no nose, no hair, no ears, thin black stick arms and legs, clean 2D hand-drawn linework, slightly organic black lines, minimal readable silhouette, emotionally readable body language, soft white or light neutral background"

Supporting stickmen may appear when the narration benefits from:

- worker
- renter
- saver
- parent
- homeowner
- shopper
- family member
- stressed employee
- person opening a bill
- person facing a financial decision
- person comparing choices
- person representing the average viewer
- person representing pressure, debt, rent, salary, fear, or hope

When supporting stickmen appear:

- the main stickman should still appear in the scene
- supporting stickmen should belong to the same minimal visual family
- supporting stickmen should be secondary to the scene's main visual idea
- supporting stickmen should not look like random stock illustrations
- supporting stickmen should not come from another visual style
- supporting stickmen should not become more visually detailed than the main stickman

Supporting stickmen may vary by:

- height
- body posture
- expression
- emotional state
- symbolic role
- simple accessory only when necessary
- scene context
- relationship to the main stickman

Supporting stickmen should remain consistent in:

- round white head
- dot eyes
- simple expressive eyebrows
- tiny simple mouth
- no nose
- no hair by default
- no ears
- thin black stick limbs
- simple black stick body
- clean 2D hand-drawn linework
- slightly organic line quality
- minimal readable silhouette
- strong emotional body language

## Expression System

The main stickman communicates emotion through:

1. eyebrows
2. tiny mouth
3. head angle
4. posture
5. arm gesture
6. body position relative to the explanatory element

The face should remain minimal.

Do not add extra facial details to make emotions clearer.

Use body language and eyebrow position instead.

Use:

- one eyebrow raised

- arms close to body

Avoid readable text inside images by default.

Do not include:

- subtitles
- captions
- paragraphs
- narration text

## Composition Rules

The visual system should follow this structure:

**MAIN STICKMAN + BIG EXPLANATORY ELEMENT**

The main stickman should be present by default.

The explanatory element should be large, simple, and visually clear.

Recommended compositions:

- main stickman on the left, big element on the right
- main stickman on the right, big element on the left
- main stickman centered under a large pressure object
- main stickman standing beside a large chart
- main stickman looking at a large symbolic object
- main stickman interacting physically with the metaphor
- main stickman in foreground, element behind
- main stickman smaller when the large idea needs to dominate
- close-up reaction shot with one simple prop
- medium shot with character and object
- full body shot when posture matters

Avoid:

- object-only scenes by default
- environment-only scenes by default
- scenes where the main stickman is absent
- scenes where the main stickman is too small to read emotionally
- cluttered multi-object layouts
- complicated perspective
- cinematic realism

---

## Camera And Framing Rules

Use:

- medium shot for explanation
- full body shot for emotional posture

- medium-wide shot for simple metaphor scenes
- centered composition for clarity
- lots of negative space

- extreme cinematic angles

- 3D perspective

- crowded scenes
